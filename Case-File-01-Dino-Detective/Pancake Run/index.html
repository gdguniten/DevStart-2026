What is Pancake Run?

A tiny keyboard-only endless runner. You are the pink cat on a breakfast field trip: obstacles rush in from the right, the pace keeps climbing, and the only goal is to survive as long as you can without face-planting.

Controls

  • Space↑ Jump
  • ↓Shift Duck

Press Space to start the run. Tap ↓ mid-air to dive back down faster.

Obstacles

Pancake stacks sit on the ground. Jump straight over them.

Bees fly at three heights: low means jump, mid means duck, high means run underneath.

Scoring

Points tick up every second you stay alive. Clear an obstacle and it makes a little pop.

Your best run is saved in this browser automatically.

The important bits

Speed rises as your score grows

Sky turns dusk at 120, night at 350
